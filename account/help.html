Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my UPI PIN?
Go to Settings → Change UPI PIN → Enter your debit card details → Set new 4-digit PIN. You can also call customer care for assistance.
What if my payment fails but money is debited?
Failed payments are automatically refunded within 7 working days. If not refunded, contact our support team with transaction details.
How secure is PaySecure?
We use 256-bit SSL encryption, two-factor authentication, and are RBI approved. Your data and money are completely secure with us.
Can I link multiple bank accounts?
Yes, you can link up to 10 bank accounts to your PaySecure account. Go to Settings → Bank Accounts → Add New Account.
What are the transaction limits?
Daily limit: ₹1,00,000 | Per transaction: ₹1,00,000 | Monthly limit: ₹20,00,000. Limits may vary based on KYC status.
